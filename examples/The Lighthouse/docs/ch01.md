Mara had kept the light on Skerry Rock for eleven winters, and in all that time the lamp had never once gone dark. She wound the clockwork at dusddk, trimmed the wick at midnight, and wrote the weather in the log at dawn — *wind NW, sea moderate, visibility good* — in the same careful hand her father had used.

The supply boat came on the first Monday of every month. This month it came on a Thursday, and it brought a stranger.

> The sea keeps what it is given, her father used to say. Be careful what you give it.
