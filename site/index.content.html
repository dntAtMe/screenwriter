Screenwriter

A writing app for screenplays, books, notes and ideas. Free, open source, and your files stay plain text.

Screenplays that format themselves

Type plain Fountain and it's laid out like a script page. int becomes INT., names in caps become character cues, and names, places and times of day are suggested as you go.

Books, chapter by chapter

A calm writing column with Markdown and word counts, and chapters you reorder in the binder or on a corkboard of index cards.

A Story Bible that reads along

Characters and locations know where they appear: scenes, speeches and words spoken, and every mention in your chapters, including inflected forms like Kacprowi.

Mind maps and corkboards

Brainstorm on a canvas of cards, or see a folder of chapters as index cards with synopses and colour labels.

Export the way it's expected

Industry-format screenplay PDF, Final Draft, manuscript-format Word and PDF, Markdown, and board images.

Nothing gets lost

Autosave, daily snapshots of every document, word-by-word comparison with earlier versions, and a project that's just a folder of text files.
